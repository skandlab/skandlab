title: Tumor Microenvironment
description: The tumor microenvironment (TME) is a multi-faceted cellular environment that both constrains the evolving tumor and plays a pivotal role in tumor progression and therapeutic response. We are developing computational approaches to profile emergent features of the TME, such as ligand-receptor signaling (<a class='txt' href='https://doi.org/10.1158/0008-5472.CAN-20-2352'>Ghoshdastider et al. 2021</a>), metabolism (<a class='txt' href='https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2022.110800'>Rohatgi et al. 2022</a> ), and tumor purity (<a class='txt' href='https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-023-04764-8'>Revkov et al. 2023</a>). We are studying biomarkers of drug response and resistance, such as TKI drug resistance in lung cancer (<a class='txt' href='https://doi.org/10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-20-4607'>Chua et al. 2021</a>) and response to immunotherapy (<a class='txt' href='https://doi.org/10.1158/2767-9764.CRC-23-0442'>Guo et al. 2024</a>).
